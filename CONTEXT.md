# Budget Bistro (working title)

A cooking-management game. The player runs a small restaurant and fills NPC **Orders** by assembling **Dishes** from **Ingredients** under a **Budget**, **Macro Targets**, and **Availability** limits.

## Language

### Game objects

**Player**:
The human user. Owns exactly one **Kitchen**.
_Avoid_: user (reserve "user" for the auth account in code), chef, account

**Kitchen**:
The Player's saved game state: **Wallet**, **Reputation**, **Day**, **Tier**, **XP**.
_Avoid_: pantry, save file, profile

**Wallet**:
The Player's current cash in the **Kitchen**.
_Avoid_: balance, money, funds

**Reputation**:
A 0-100 score that rises on good **Reviews** and falls on rejected or failed **Orders**. Zero ends the game.
_Avoid_: rating, clout, fame

**Tier**:
The Player's restaurant level (Food Truck, Diner, Bistro, Fine Dining...). Unlocks harder **Customers** and new **Constraint Types**.
_Avoid_: level, rank

**Day**:
One turn of the game. Advanced only by **Next Day**.
_Avoid_: tick, turn, cycle

**Ingredient**:
A purchasable food item with a base price, per-100g macros, a category, and a stock limit.
_Avoid_: item, product, component

**Market Price**:
An Ingredient's current price = base price x active **Market Event** multipliers.
_Avoid_: cost, current price

**Dish**:
A Player-assembled combination of Ingredients (with quantities) submitted against one Order.
_Avoid_: recipe (a recipe is a saved Dish in the Cookbook), meal, plate

**Dish Ingredient**:
One line of a Dish: which Ingredient and how many grams (`quantity_g`).
_Avoid_: dish item, line item

**Cookbook**:
The Player's saved Dishes that scored 4+ stars; can be published for passive income.
_Avoid_: menu, recipe book

### Customers and judging

**Customer**:
An NPC generated by RNG with a profile (job, diet, favorite and blacklisted Ingredients, patience).
_Avoid_: NPC (in code), client, user

**Order**:
A Customer's request for one Dish, carrying a **Budget**, **Macro Targets**, and preferences.
_Avoid_: ticket, commission, request

**Budget**:
The maximum total ingredient cost an Order allows.
_Avoid_: price limit, cap

**Macro Targets**:
Per-Order targets for protein, carbs, and fat in grams, each with a **Tolerance**.
_Avoid_: nutrition goals, macros (alone is fine in UI copy only)

**Tolerance**:
The allowed percentage deviation from a Macro Target (shrinks as Tier rises).
_Avoid_: margin, error band

**Score**:
The 1-5 star result of judging a Dish. Computed by the **Scoring Engine**.
_Avoid_: grade, rating

**Scoring Engine**:
The single deep module that turns (Order, Dish, Market Prices, seed) into a Score and Review text.
_Avoid_: grader, judge service

**Luck Roll**:
A seeded d20 applied to the Score after fixed math. Never hides a hard failure (over budget or blacklisted Ingredient).
_Avoid_: dice, random bonus

**Review**:
Auto-generated Customer text stored against a Dish. One Dish has many Reviews over time (e.g. re-serve, critic follow-up).
_Avoid_: feedback, comment

### World and economy

**Market Event**:
A news item from the curated event library that multiplies prices for an Ingredient category for N Days.
_Avoid_: news, shock, random event

**Next Day**:
The non-RESTful action that advances the Day: rolls Market Events, generates Orders, charges rent.
_Avoid_: end turn, tick

**Rent**:
Fixed Wallet deduction each Next Day; unpayable Rent ends the game.
_Avoid_: upkeep, fee

**Supplier**:
An NPC that sells an Ingredient category at a discount once the Player's **Trust** with them is high enough.
_Avoid_: vendor, merchant

**Trust**:
Per-Player-per-Supplier number on the join row. Earned by serving that Supplier's Orders well.
_Avoid_: relationship, friendship

**Constraint Type**:
A kind of rule an Order can impose (budget, macro, allergen, availability, multi-course, waste). Each Tier introduces a new one.
_Avoid_: modifier, rule

**Cosmetic**:
A purchasable or earnable visual item (theme, plate style, apron). Never affects Scores.
_Avoid_: skin, unlockable

## Relationships

- A **Player** has exactly one **Kitchen** (1:1)
- A **Customer** has many **Orders**; an **Order** has many **Dishes** (re-attempts)
- A **Dish** has many **Reviews** (1:N)
- A **Dish** has many **Ingredients** through **Dish Ingredients** (M:N, unique field `quantity_g`)
- A **Kitchen** owns many **Cosmetics** through a join table (M:N, unique field `equipped`)
- A **Kitchen** has **Trust** with many **Suppliers** (M:N, unique field `trust`)

## Flagged ambiguities

- "user" vs "Player": DB `users` table = auth identity (stretch); **Player** = the human in game terms. They map 1:1.
- "recipe" vs "Dish": a Dish is one submission; a recipe exists only once saved to the **Cookbook**.
- "pantry" was dropped: **Kitchen** holds state, **Ingredient** stock is global per Day.
